# Sales & Operations Performance Management Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze sales and operational performance and provide management with a centralized view of key business metrics.

## Business Problem

Organizations often manage sales and operational information across multiple datasets, making it difficult to monitor overall performance and identify issues quickly.

This dashboard was created to bring important business metrics together and support data-driven decision-making.

## Business Objectives

The dashboard analyzes:

* Sales Performance vs Target
* Employee Performance
* Regional Performance
* Lead Conversion
* Profitability
* Payment Exceptions
* Delivery Exceptions
* Employee-Level Sales Trends

## Project Workflow

1. Imported data from Microsoft Excel.
2. Cleaned and transformed the data using Power Query.
3. Created a data model and established relationships between tables.
4. Developed DAX measures and calculated columns.
5. Created KPIs for important business metrics.
6. Designed interactive visualizations and dashboard pages.
7. Added slicers, drill-through functionality, navigation, and synchronized filters.

## Business Value

The dashboard helps users monitor business performance, identify performance gaps, analyze trends, and support better operational and management decisions.
