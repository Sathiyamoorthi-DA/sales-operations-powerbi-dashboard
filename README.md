# 📊 Sales Operations Performance Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Sales Operations Performance Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI business intelligence solution designed to analyze sales performance, employee performance, regional performance, order operations, profitability, and target achievement.

The dashboard transforms sales and operational data into actionable business insights through interactive KPIs, charts, slicers, navigation, employee-level analysis, regional analysis, and detailed order information.

The goal of this project is to provide a centralized analytical view of sales and operations performance and help management monitor business growth, employee performance, regional sales, profitability, and operational activities.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard is designed to answer key sales and operations business questions:

- What are the total sales and total orders?
- How is actual sales performing against the sales target?
- What percentage of the sales target has been achieved?
- What is the current target gap?
- How is sales and profit changing month over month?
- Which employees generate the highest sales?
- Which regions generate the highest sales?
- Which product categories contribute the most to sales?
- Which products generate the highest sales?
- What is the overall profit and profit margin?
- What is the lead conversion rate?
- How many orders are delayed or pending payment?
- Which lead sources generate the most converted orders?
- What is the distribution of payment and delivery status?
- How is each employee performing against their sales target?
- What are the details of individual orders?

## 📈 Key KPIs

The dashboard tracks important sales and operations performance indicators such as:

| KPI | Purpose |

| Total Orders | Monitor order volume |
| Sales Target | Monitor expected sales performance |
| Target Achievement % | Measure sales achievement against target |
| Target Gap | Identify the difference between actual sales and target |
| Conversion Rate | Monitor lead conversion performance |
| Total Profit | Monitor overall profitability |
| Profit Margin | Measure profitability relative to sales |
| Converted Leads | Monitor successful lead conversions |
| Delayed Orders | Monitor delivery issues |
| Pending Payment | Monitor outstanding payment orders |
